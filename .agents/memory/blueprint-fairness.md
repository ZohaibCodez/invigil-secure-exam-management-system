---
name: Blueprint fairness limits
description: Scope and evidence limits for randomized examination papers
---

Treat blueprint matching as coverage comparability, not psychometric equivalence.
Examiner difficulty labels and small demo samples must never be presented as
validated calibration, and observed performance must not silently adjust grades.

**Why:** The user explicitly required these evidence and grade-integrity limits.

**How to apply:** Preserve these distinctions in future analytics, form-generation
changes, reports and product claims. If research-based equating is considered
later, it requires a separate explicit policy and validation process.
